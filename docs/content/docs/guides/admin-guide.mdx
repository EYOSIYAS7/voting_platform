# Election Administrator Guide

Election Administrators (`ELECTION_ADMIN`) and System Administrators (`SYSTEM_ADMIN`) manage election creation, candidate nominations, voter eligibility rules, and blockchain synchronization.

---

## 1. Creating a New Election

1. Navigate to **Managed Elections** (`/my-elections`).
2. Click **Create New Election**.
3. Fill in the election parameters:
   - **Title & Description**: Detailed summary of the election purpose.
   - **Ballot Type**:
     - *Organization-Wide*: Open to all eligible employees across the enterprise.
     - *Unit-Scoped*: Restricted to a specific department or division.
   - **Maximum Candidates**: Cap on the number of approved candidates allowed on the ballot.
   - **Timeline Configuration**:
     - *Registration Deadline*: When candidate nominations close.
     - *Voting Start Date*: When live voting begins (auto-starts if untouched).
     - *Voting End Date*: When voting concludes (auto-ends if untouched).
4. Click **Create Election**. The election is created in `DRAFT` status.

---

## 2. Configuring Eligibility Rules

1. Open the election management workspace (`/my-elections/:id`).
2. Select the **Rules & Voter Roll** tab.
3. Click **Add Rule**:
   - **Department Scope**: Select an organizational unit and toggle whether to include all sub-departments.
   - **Allowed Positions**: Select specific job roles eligible to vote.
   - **Minimum Tenure**: Specify minimum service months required.
4. Click **Compute Voter Roll**:
   - The eligibility engine scans all active employees against the configured rules.
   - Eligible employee records are generated in the voter roll table.

---

## 3. Managing Candidate Applications

1. Switch to the **Candidates** tab in the management workspace.
2. **Reviewing Applications**:
   - For pending nominations, click **Approve** to accept the nominee onto the ballot, or **Reject** (with a mandatory feedback reason).
3. **Manual Nomination**:
   - Administrators can directly nominate an eligible employee using the **Nominate Candidate** button.

---

## 4. Starting Live Voting (Unified Action)

When the registration and review phases are complete:
1. Click **Start Live Voting & Publish to Chain**.
2. This single action atomically:
   - Updates the election status to `ACTIVE`.
   - Deploys the election smart contract on-chain (`onChainElectionId`).
   - Assigns on-chain ballot slots to all approved candidates (`onChainCandidateIndex`).
   - Finalizes the voter roll.

---

## 5. Concluding Elections & Reviewing Results

- Elections automatically conclude once the configured `endDate` is reached.
- Alternatively, administrators can click **End Voting & Conclude** to close voting ahead of schedule.
- Once ended, immutable cryptographic tallies are fetched directly from the smart contract and displayed on the results dashboard.
