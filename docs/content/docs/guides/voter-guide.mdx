# Voter & Employee Guide

This guide explains how employees participate in elections, submit candidate nominations, cast ballots, and verify their cryptographic vote receipts.

---

## 1. Browsing Elections

1. Log in with your employee email and password.
2. Navigate to **Elections** (`/elections`).
3. Filter by election status:
   - **Active**: Elections currently open for voting.
   - **Upcoming**: Elections scheduled to start soon or in candidate review.
   - **Registration**: Elections currently accepting candidate self-nominations.
   - **Closed**: Concluded elections with finalized results.

---

## 2. Registering as a Candidate

During the `REGISTRATION` phase of an election:
1. Open the election details page (`/elections/:id`).
2. If self-nominations are open, the **Register as a Candidate** card will appear.
3. Fill in your campaign details:
   - **Campaign Statement**: Summary of your goals and qualifications.
   - **Slogan**: Catchy slogan displayed under your candidate card.
4. Click **Submit Candidacy**.
5. Your application enters `PENDING` review. Once an Election Administrator reviews your application, your status will update to `APPROVED` or `REJECTED` (with feedback).

---

## 3. Casting Your Ballot

When an election is in the `ACTIVE` (Live Voting) phase:
1. Open the election details page (`/elections/:id`).
2. The page verifies your eligibility:
   - If eligible, you will see the active candidate cards with the option to select a nominee.
   - If ineligible, an explanatory banner details the required department or role rules.
3. Review the candidate statements and platforms.
4. Click **Select Candidate**, then click **Confirm & Submit Vote**.
5. The transaction is relayed on-chain via the zero-gas server relayer.

---

## 4. Cryptographic Vote Receipt & Audit

Immediately after submitting your ballot:
1. A green confirmation banner displays your official **Transaction Hash** (`txHash`).
2. You can click **Verify on Blockchain Explorer** to view the mined block receipt on the public ledger.
3. The receipt confirms:
   - Your ballot was officially recorded within the legal voting window.
   - The choice you voted for remains confidential and cannot be viewed by third parties.
